1. Parties, purpose, and scope

This Refund Policy (“Policy”) explains the cancellation and voluntary-refund conditions applied by Kılıç Efe Çelebi, who provides services under the name TA Studios (“TA Studios”). TA Studios is the community and service name used by Kılıç Efe Çelebi; it is not a separate company or legal entity. Sentanel is the verified Discord bot affiliated with TA Studios. This Policy applies to Sentanel Star and Sentanel Star Plus purchases and is read together with the Terms of Service and any order-specific Distance Sales Pre-Information Form and Distance Sales Agreement. It does not restrict the right of withdrawal, rights concerning defective or undelivered services, or other mandatory consumer rights.

2. Sales channels and purchase types

Stripe processes web payments made through ta-studios.com; Discord Store processes purchases made inside Discord. A subscription may renew automatically. A gift code or direct gift is a one-time purchase and does not renew. Refund, cancellation, and completion follow the rules of the relevant payment channel.

3. Cancellation, delivery, and withdrawal

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewal; it does not automatically refund a past payment or end the current paid period early. Before payment, immediate delivery of the digital service or gift code is requested through separate, unticked declarations. The order-specific document explains how those declarations may affect withdrawal rights; other statutory rights remain.

4. When a voluntary refund may be considered

A voluntary refund may be considered where payment was taken but access was never delivered, core paid features were unavailable for at least seven continuous calendar days due to TA Studios, a duplicate payment occurred, or a verified system error caused the issue. Choosing the wrong plan, duration, account, or recipient; changing your mind after use; a valid rules violation; losing access to a linked account; or assuming cancellation refunds a past payment is generally not a voluntary-refund reason. Each case is reviewed separately.

5. Request

Submit the request through Live Support or [email protected]. The order number, approximate transaction time, and a short explanation may be enough. Do not send a card number, card security code, password, or one-time sign-in code. A verified account email is not required.

6. Review and payment outcome

Opening a request does not immediately end access. Access changes only after the payment provider confirms a successful refund. A pending, failed, or partial transaction is not a full refund. For duplicate payment, only the excess charge is refunded. Fraudulent requests or payment abuse may be reviewed; the final decision is not based only on an automated outcome.

7. Effect of a refund on paid features

A successful full refund ends only the paid access linked to the refunded purchase source. If other valid paid access from Stripe, Discord Store, the App Store, or a gift exists, access and the related role may remain. A refund may not always stop automatic renewal by itself; cancel the subscription separately through its sales channel if no new charge is wanted.

8. Statutory rights and contact

Mandatory consumer rights and any legally competent Consumer Arbitration Committee, Consumer Court, or other available authority remain available. For refund or cancellation questions, use Live Support or [email protected].
