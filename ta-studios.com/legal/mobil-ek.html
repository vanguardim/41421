1. Parties, purpose, and main documents

This Mobile Application Supplementary Information (“Mobile Supplement”) explains additional conditions and information specific to the iOS and Android apps provided by Kılıç Efe Çelebi, who provides services under the name TA Studios (“TA Studios”), for people using those apps (“you”). TA Studios is the community and service name used by Kılıç Efe Çelebi; it is not a separate company or legal entity. Sentanel is the verified Discord bot affiliated with TA Studios. The Mobile Supplement complements the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy and KVKK Information Notice; it does not replace them.

2. Sessions and device preferences

The apps may use sessions, secure sign-in, language, appearance, and limited preferences. The app does not receive a Discord or Roblox password; permission is given on the relevant service’s secure screen. Linked features may stop after sign-out or unlinking.

3. iOS notifications

iOS notifications may be delivered through Apple’s notification service. The device record required to send notifications and limited app, device, and operating-system details are used. A preview may appear on the lock screen; permission can be turned off in device settings.

4. iOS in-app purchase

Where the iOS app offers an App Store purchase, payment, renewal, cancellation, and Apple-provided refunds follow App Store rules. When the App Store purchase is completed successfully, the purchased Paid Features may be assigned to the relevant Discord account; a secure link may be needed for this if the person was not signed in. Stripe web payment and Discord Store purchases are separate channels.

5. Android

The Android app may use sessions, required cookies, language, and limited preferences. Notifications and purchases through Google Play are not currently offered in the Android app.

6. Account links, notifications, and deletion

After Discord unlinking or completed account deletion, the device record required to send app notifications is removed or made unusable. Account deletion does not itself cancel a payment or subscription with the relevant sales channel and does not by itself create a refund; the main documents provide details.

7. Options that vary by store

Purchase and notification options may vary by app version, store region, and store rules. An option on the website is not guaranteed to appear in every mobile store. The channel, price, duration, renewal, and document information shown for the transaction control.

8. Store disclosures and contact

Store privacy disclosures reflect the data use described here for mobile sessions, account links, iOS notifications, App Store purchase records, support, payment, and account deletion. Use Live Support or [email protected] for questions.
