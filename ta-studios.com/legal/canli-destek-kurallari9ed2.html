Live Support Usage Rules

CurrentVersion3.0EffectiveAugust 1, 2026Last updatedAugust 1, 2026LanguageEnglish translation; Turkish is the primary text

1. Parties, purpose, and scope

These Live Support Usage Rules (“Rules”) set the rules for people (“you”) using Live Support provided by Kılıç Efe Çelebi, who provides services under the name TA Studios (“TA Studios”). TA Studios is the community and service name used by Kılıç Efe Çelebi; it is not a separate company or legal entity. Sentanel is the verified Discord bot affiliated with TA Studios. These Rules apply together with the Terms of Service. Conversations are not public; only the person who opened a conversation and authorized people who need access for their duties may list or read it or download its files.

2. Account and accurate information

Link your own Discord account and, where needed, Roblox account. The name, avatar, group, rank, and friendship result at the time a conversation opens may be retained as limited support history; this information is not by itself conclusive proof or a reason for automatic enforcement. Provide the accurate information needed to resolve the issue.

3. Safe use

Spam, harassment, threats, forged documents, another person’s private information, harmful files, category abuse, and attempts to bypass security are prohibited. Criticism or a complaint is not itself a violation. Do not send card numbers, passwords, identity documents, or one-time sign-in codes.

4. File security

The file limit is 20 MB. Uploaded files are checked for security through VirusTotal. A file may be rejected if the check cannot be completed or the file is not found safe. Only people authorized to view the conversation may download it. Do not upload private, confidential, or unauthorized content.

5. Category blocking and appeal

A staff member may impose a reasoned and proportionate block only for a category they can access. The block does not automatically close the whole account and is not applied indefinitely based only on an automated outcome. You receive a safe general reason and may request review through [email protected].

6. Retention and deletion

A support conversation, its messages, and files are kept until you or an authorized person deletes them or account deletion is completed; archiving a conversation is not deletion. The Roblox account view and notice email associated with a closed conversation are kept for up to 365 days, and records about the delivery status of sent support notices for up to 180 days. Only the relevant record may be kept longer while required for an active dispute, security review, or legal obligation.

7. Notices and contact

A limited, non-advertising email may be sent when a conversation opens, staff reply, or the conversation closes or is deleted. Replying to the email does not add a message to the conversation; use the secure support link. For a rule, access, or block appeal, use Live Support or [email protected]; a verified email is not required.
