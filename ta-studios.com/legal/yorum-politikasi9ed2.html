Comment Policy

CurrentVersion3.1EffectiveAugust 10, 2026Last updatedAugust 10, 2026LanguageEnglish translation; Turkish is the primary text

1. Parties, purpose, and scope

This Comment Policy (“Policy”) sets the rules that people using the Wiki comment area provided by Kılıç Efe Çelebi, who provides services under the name TA Studios (“TA Studios”), must follow. TA Studios is the community and service name used by Kılıç Efe Çelebi; it is not a separate company or legal entity. This Policy applies together with the Terms of Service. Wiki comments are used through a securely linked Discord account.

2. Participation and account responsibility

Comments are for people aged 13 or over who are authorized to use the linked account. Protect your account, do not impersonate another person, and do not exchange personal contact details with minors. Criticism, complaints, and differing views are not violations by themselves.

3. Contribution standard

  • Write respectfully, stay on topic, and add genuine value to the discussion.
  • Avoid excessive repetition, capital letters, formatting abuse, and disruptive posts.
  • Share only content that you have the right to publish.

4. Prohibited content and conduct

  • Hate, discrimination, harassment, threats, blackmail, targeting, or encouragement of violence.
  • Illegal content, sexual-abuse material, or encouragement of harm to yourself or others.
  • Unauthorized addresses, emails, phone numbers, identity, account data, or other personal information.
  • Impersonation, false authority, fraud, spam, undisclosed advertising, or mass comments.
  • Malware, security-bypass instructions, suspicious downloads, or hidden redirects.

5. Links, media, and privacy

Share only relevant and safe links. Shortened or hidden-destination links may be removed. Your Discord name, avatar, comment, and date may be public. The unique user identifier assigned to your account by Discord (Discord user identifier/ID) is not shown in the public interface unless necessary. Do not post sensitive information. See the Privacy Policy and KVKK Information Notice for details.

6. Moderation and enforcement

TA Studios applies zero tolerance to harassment, targeted harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, encouragement of violence, sexual exploitation, illegal content, spam, fraud, and severe abuse. Content may be blocked server-side or removed, a discussion may be locked, or comment access may be restricted temporarily or permanently. Reminders, warnings, and temporary restrictions may be used; serious or repeated violations need not follow each step in order. Decisions must be limited to community safety, service continuity, protection of rights, and legal obligations.

7. Editing, deletion, and retention

The author may edit a comment during the first hour. Old text is not shown publicly and is deleted after 30 days. Deletion by the author or a moderator is irreversible: text, author information, and old versions are removed. With no replies, the comment is deleted completely; with replies, an identity-free and content-free “Deleted comment” placeholder may remain to preserve the conversation order.

8. Reports and appeals

A problematic comment may be reported through the in-app report endpoint; the reported content is hidden immediately from the reporter. Blocking a user immediately hides that user's existing and future public content from the blocker and reports the source content to the moderation queue. Every report enters the developer-notification system and is reviewed within 24 hours. Live Support is also available. To request a moderation review, send the comment link and a short reason to [email protected]. Permanently deleted content cannot be restored.

9. Content rights, changes, and contact

You retain ownership of your content. You give TA Studios only the limited permission necessary to host it, show it in the public area you selected, operate security and moderation, and keep required backups. Material changes are announced in advance where possible; urgent security or legal changes may apply sooner with a reason. Use Live Support or [email protected] for questions.
