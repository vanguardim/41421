1. Data controller, data subject, and scope of this notice

This Privacy Policy and KVKK Information Notice (“Information Notice”) is provided by Kılıç Efe Çelebi (“Data Controller”), who offers the services under the name TA Studios, and explains to people using the services (“you” or the “data subject”) the purposes, methods, and legal grounds for processing personal data, the people with whom it may be shared, how long it is retained, and your rights.

TA Studios is the community and service name used by the Data Controller; it is not a separate company or legal entity. Sentanel is the verified Discord bot affiliated with TA Studios. This Information Notice is not a contract, a term of service, or an explicit-consent declaration. The location is Türkiye / Ordu / Altınordu. Use Live Support or [email protected] for a request. The normal process does not ask for an identity document, password, or full card details.

2. Information we process

To recognize your account and provide the Service, the Data Controller may process the unique user identifier assigned to your account by Discord (Discord user identifier/ID), username, display name, avatar, server membership, role, nickname, and account-related email. Session and preference information, as well as the plan, start and end dates of Paid Features, purchase channel, the Discord server where they will be used, and assignment records may also be processed.

Sentanel processes message content only when required for the command, moderation feature, game feature, support conversation, or Sentanel AI feature you use. Operation records that do not require content do not contain message text, and messages are not sent on your behalf.

Required cookies and device storage may be used for sessions, sign-in security, language, appearance, and notification preferences. Google Analytics, advertising cookies, behavioural tracking, and sale of personal data for advertising are not used. The IP address and device and browser information used during the connection may be processed temporarily for connection and security; this information is not kept in permanent web records.

3. Discord and Roblox account links

A secure Discord account link is used for profiles, Wiki, comments, Sentanel transactions, and staff areas. Team members shown in Meet the Team may separately and optionally allow display of connections they made visible on Discord. The visible connection type, account name or identifier, verification status, and a constructible profile link are used only while the relevant information is displayed and are not stored permanently.

A secure Roblox account link is used in Live Support to match the linked Discord account and, where needed, the Roblox account, and to check a limited group or rank result when necessary. TA Studios does not receive the Roblox password, payment details, or Robux information. See the Discord Privacy Policy and Roblox Privacy and Cookie Policy for details.

4. Profile, Wiki, and comment information

Profiles and custom profiles may contain text, links, images, files, appearance, and dates; Wiki and comments may contain content, dates, and the linked-account view. When a comment is deleted, its text, author information, and old versions are removed; where replies exist, only an identity-free “Deleted comment” placeholder may remain to preserve order.

5. Live Support and uploaded files

Live Support may store messages, files, category, opening, reply, and closing records, information about the linked Discord account and, where needed, the Roblox account, and the account-related email only for notices. A notice email is limited to the conversation number, state, sender name, short preview, and secure support link; uploaded files are not attached to the email.

Files uploaded through Live Support may be sent to VirusTotal for a security check. Submitted files may be retained by VirusTotal or shared with security service providers. Therefore, do not upload private, confidential, or unauthorized files.

6. Purchase, gift, and payment information

TA Studios may process the order number, plan, duration, purchase type, recipient, the Discord server where paid features will be used, amount, currency, coupon, renewal, status of payment, cancellation, and refund, contract, and declarations. TA Studios does not store the card number or card security code. Stripe processes web payment, while Discord Store processes purchases inside Discord. Where App Store purchase is offered in the iOS app, the App Store purchase record and information required to assign paid features to the relevant account may be processed. For web payments by people aged 13–17, the parent or legal representative’s name, email, the Discord account for which the purchase is made, and order approval are processed.

7. Mobile app and notification information

Mobile sessions, required preferences, the device record required to send notifications, and limited app, device, and operating-system details may be used. Notification permission can be turned off in device settings. See the Mobile Application Supplementary Information for details.

8. Why and on which legal grounds do we process data?

Data is used for account and session security, profile and content publication, operating Sentanel features, assigning Paid Features to the relevant account, gifts, payments, refunds, Live Support, notifications, abuse and fraud prevention, error resolution, rights requests, and legal obligations. It is collected from information you provide, secure connection screens of linked services, payment outcomes, use of Sentanel and the site, support or content forms, and security checks.

For each purpose, the Data Controller considers performance of the service relationship, legal obligations, establishment, exercise, or protection of a right, or a proportionate legitimate interest. Separate, specific, and revocable explicit consent is requested only where necessary for an optional operation.

9. Who do we share data with?

Only necessary data may be shared with Discord for sign-in, roles, and Discord Store transactions; Roblox for account matching; Stripe for web payment; Cloudflare for traffic security; a restricted GitHub backup; VirusTotal for file security; Apple for iOS purchases and notifications; the email service used for support notices; authorized people who need access for their duties; and competent authorities following a lawful request.

10. Information processed outside Türkiye

Some of Discord, Roblox, Stripe, Cloudflare, GitHub, VirusTotal, Apple, and the email services used may process data outside Türkiye. Cross-border transfers are carried out to the extent that they must rely on a lawful method under the applicable personal-data-protection legislation.

11. Retention and deletion periods

Profile data is kept while the account is open, connection data while the link remains, and access data relating to Paid Features while those features remain. Old edited-comment text is kept for up to 30 days, and records about the delivery status of sent support notices for up to 180 days. The Roblox account view and notice email associated with a closed support conversation are kept for up to 365 days. A support conversation, its messages, and files are kept until you or an authorized person deletes them or account deletion is completed; archiving a conversation is not deletion. Payment and refund evidence and finalized purchase documents are kept for the period required for legal obligations and for establishing, exercising, or protecting rights. Only the relevant record may be kept longer while required for an active dispute, security review, or legal obligation.

Deleted information may remain in restricted backups for as long as the backups are retained; completed deletions are reapplied after a backup restore. The Service is not offered to children under 13; a parent or legal-representative flow is used for web payments by people aged 13–17.

12. Your rights and requests

You may unlink an account, turn off notification permission on the device, and request removal of profile or comment content. Limited payment, dispute, security, or legally required records may remain for the necessary period after unlinking.

Under KVKK, you may ask whether personal data is processed; request information, purpose, and proper-use details; learn recipients; request correction and, where conditions apply, deletion or destruction and notice to recipients; object to an adverse result produced only by automated analysis; and seek compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

Submit a request through Live Support, [email protected], or another legally valid method. Unnecessary identity documents, passwords, or payment details are not requested. A request is concluded as soon as possible and no later than 30 days. Material changes are announced in advance where possible; presenting this notice is not recorded as acceptance.
